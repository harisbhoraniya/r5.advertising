CABLE CLEATS
NAEPLΓÇÖs Cable Cleats are designed to securely hold and support
your cables within the cable tray system under all operating
conditions. More importantly, they protect the installation during a
short circuit. Short circuits are an unfortunate reality, and when one
occurs, the resulting electromagnetic forces can be destructive and
dangerous to both the cables and the wider cable system. Cable
cleats act as one of the first lines of defence, helping to safeguard
personnel, cables and the entire cable system.
Construction of the Cable
Cleats
The cable cleats are made of
a composite material and are
supported by a metallic frame of
50 mm ├ù 2.1 mm, manufactured
from marine-grade, non-
magnetic 316L stainless steel.
For closure, 316L stainless steel
bolts of size M8, M10 or M12 are
used along with nylon lock nuts,
ensuring a secure and corrosion-
resistant fit. The integral pad
is made of a low smoke, low
fume and halogen-free material,
which makes the cleats suitable
for use in enclosed and safety-
critical installations. Installation
requires only a wrench, and the
mounting bolt is provided along
with each cable cleat.
FEATURES & SPECIFICATIONS
Performance of the Cable Cleats
The cable cleats are tested for their
ability to withstand electromechanical
forces caused by short-circuit
conditions. Using a test cable with
an outer diameter of 35 mm, they
withstand a peak current of 180 kA at
300 mm spacing (Clause 9.5.2) and 125
kA at 600 mm spacing (Clause 9.5.3).
In the lateral load test, they achieve
an average of 25 kg, and they pass the
axial load test.
The cleats operate reliably across a
temperature range of -40┬░C to +60┬░C
and have a very heavy resistance to
impact. They also withstand the needle
flame test for 30 seconds and show
resistance to UV light exposure for
1000 hours, making them suitable for
both indoor and outdoor applications.
